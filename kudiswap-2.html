About KudiSwap

KudiSwap is a peer to peer marketplace community where members can sell pre loved items they no longer use and buy affordable items from others.

Our goal is simple: help you save money, reduce waste, and connect with people in a safe and friendly environment. Every listing is posted directly by community members in our WhatsApp and Telegram channels, so you always know who you are dealing with.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is KudiSwap free to use?
Yes, joining the community and browsing items is free. You only pay when you choose to buy an item from a seller.
Is KudiSwap safe?
We built KudiSwap around trust and safety. Sales happen through our community channels, and we recommend meeting in public places for handovers.
How do I buy or sell an item?
Join the WhatsApp or Telegram channel. To sell, post what you have with a price. To buy, browse what others have posted and message the seller directly to agree on terms.
Can I negotiate the price with a seller?
Yes. Prices are set by each seller, but you are welcome to message them directly and negotiate before completing a purchase.
